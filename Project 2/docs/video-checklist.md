# Video and transcript checklist

Use three separate YouTube recordings. Requirements come from the [Project 2 assignment](../sources/Assignment.md), the [course checklist](https://github.com/CinderZhang/FIN43900-Fall2026/blob/main/projects/major-project-student-checklist.md) and the student's Project 1 feedback. Face visibility applies to every video.

| Video | Purpose | Hard cap | Suggested rehearsal target |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Explain how the system and your thinking changed; show one AI output you personally checked | 3:00 | 2:40 |
| 2 | Explain the code that produces the decision, its checks and the authentic locked experiment | 5:00 | 4:40 |
| 3 | Open with the running app; present the mandate, comparisons, stresses, recommendation and reversal conditions | 5:00 | 4:40 |

The targets leave editing room; the caps include the opening statements. Keep your face visibly in frame while showing readable app/code content. A camera-off avatar does not satisfy the feedback.

## Before recording

- Review the analysis yourself. Open each video with the AI tool and its uses, your truthful ownership of the analysis/decision, and the education statement required by the assignment.
- Use the final documented version. Describe modeled portfolio checks separately from the unmet deployment conditions.
- Describe only checks or outside review that actually happened. An AI-written script is not evidence of either.
- In Video 3, have the live app visible at the opening while delivering the required statements.

## After recording

- Measure the final exported duration, including any introduction or ending, against the cap.
- Enable transcripts/captions on YouTube. Correct tickers, finance terms, names, attribution, dates, material numbers and percentages against the recording and final results.
- Save the actual corrected transcripts as `docs/transcript-video-1.txt`, `docs/transcript-video-2.txt` and `docs/transcript-video-3.txt`. Include timestamps aligned to the final edit. Do not use the planned script as a transcript.
- Add titles, durations, timestamp indices, transcript GitHub links and YouTube URLs to the manifest. Check video access while logged out and repository access for the instructional team.
- Attach or link all three corrected transcripts in Brightspace. Record the access check and upload receipt.

No videos or transcripts were supplied for this revision. Transcript files will be created from recordings, not from invented speech.
